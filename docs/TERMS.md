## Your trees and your Geni account

- Your trees are stored on your device. You are responsible for backups, for example by exporting a GEDCOM file.
- Logging in with Geni is optional. You log in on geni.com; Treekin never sees your Geni password.
- Use of your Geni account and of Geni's data stays subject to Geni's own terms and privacy policy: https://www.geni.com/home/privacy
- You are responsible for what you add to a tree, including information about living relatives. Share only what you are allowed to share.

## What can change on Geni

While you are logged in, the following local changes are sent to Geni under your account: new, edited or deleted profiles, relationships and photos (photos only when the photo upload option is on). Changes made on Geni can also be loaded into a Treekin tree. Deletions and edits made through the app can affect profiles that other Geni users see or manage. Check changes before you let them sync.

Privacy details: [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md).

## Disconnecting

- Log out in the Geni account screen to remove the token from the device.
- Revoke Treekin's access in your Geni account settings at any time.
- Data already on Geni stays there until you delete it on Geni. Deleting a tree in Treekin, or uninstalling the app, removes local data only.
